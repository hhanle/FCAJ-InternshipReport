### Overall Evaluation

**1. Learning and Working Environment**
The environment in the Workforce Bootcamp – First Cloud AI Journey program is dynamic, open, and provides favorable conditions for learning. Throughout the program, I had the opportunity to acquire new knowledge while gaining hands-on experience with AWS services through labs and group projects. Participants also regularly exchanged knowledge and supported each other when facing difficulties. However, I think the program could include more networking or knowledge-sharing activities between teams to strengthen connections among participants.

**2. Support from Mentors / Organizers**
The mentors and organizers provided good support throughout the program, from providing learning materials and guidance to helping resolve issues that arose during the learning process. I particularly appreciated that the mentors not only provided instructions but also encouraged students to independently research, experiment, and find solutions before seeking assistance. This helped me develop stronger self-learning skills and become more proactive when dealing with technical problems.

**3. Relevance of the Internship Content to My Major**
The program content is closely related to the knowledge of information technology and software development that I have learned at university. In addition, the program allowed me to gain deeper exposure to Cloud Computing and AWS services such as EC2, S3, RDS, Lambda, DynamoDB, VPC, CloudFormation, and AWS CDK. The combination of fundamental knowledge and hands-on practice helped me better understand how these services are deployed and integrated within a system.

**4. Learning Opportunities & Skill Development**
The program provided many opportunities for me to develop both technical knowledge and soft skills. Through labs, workshops, and group projects, I improved my ability to research technical documentation, deploy AWS infrastructure, troubleshoot issues, and work with new technologies. The group project also helped me strengthen my teamwork, task allocation, idea presentation, and problem-solving skills.

**5. Culture & Team Spirit**
The environment within the program made it comfortable to communicate and learn from others. Team members were able to share knowledge, support each other when encountering technical issues, and discuss solutions together. The process of working on the project and workshop helped me better understand the importance of collaboration, individual responsibility, and communication within a technical team.

**6. Content and Activities for Interns**
The program provides a diverse learning roadmap, covering Networking, Compute, Storage, Database, and Security, as well as Serverless and Infrastructure as Code. In addition to technical content, participating in workshops and events such as FCAJ – AGENTIC AI BUILD WEEK gave me a broader perspective on how Cloud and AI technologies can be applied to build practical products. However, I would appreciate having more review sessions or in-depth discussions after each learning phase so that students can consolidate and better understand the knowledge they have acquired.
